When most of your readers pay for data by the megabyte, page weight stops being a vanity metric.

- Ship HTML first; add JavaScript only where interaction needs it.
- Size every image explicitly and let the framework serve the smallest one that fits.
- Load embeds such as videos only after the reader asks for them.
- Self-host fonts and limit yourself to one or two families.
