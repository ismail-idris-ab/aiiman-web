Most product advice assumes a user with fast data, a bank app and an English-language interface. Many traders in Northern Nigeria have none of those by default, and that is exactly why designing for them first is useful.

## Constraints are a spec

Cash-heavy trade, trust built face to face, voice over text, and data that costs real money. Each one rules out a whole class of features and forces the product to do one job clearly.

## What this changes in practice

- Pages and screens that work on a slow, metered connection.
- Confirmation that a person can hear, not just read.
- Flows that respect how trust already works in the market.

## Why it travels

A product that works under these constraints usually works everywhere else too. The reverse is rarely true.
