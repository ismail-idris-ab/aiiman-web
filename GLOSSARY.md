# Aiiman

Personal publishing site of Ismail Idris (Aiiman): one public archive for everything he writes or makes, aimed at developers and builders in Nigeria and Africa.

## Archive

**Item**:
Anything that appears in the archive, feeds, or search. Every Item is either an Entry or a Link.
_Avoid_: Post, content, piece

**Entry**:
An Item written and hosted on this site, with its own page.
_Avoid_: Post, article, blog post

**Link**:
An Item hosted elsewhere (a video, podcast, talk, or guest post) that appears in the archive but has no page of its own.
_Avoid_: External post, bookmark

**Type**:
The kind of an Item. Entries are essay, note, tutorial, or snippet; Links are video, podcast, talk, or guest-post.
_Avoid_: Category, format

## Entry types

**Essay**:
A long-form Entry that argues a point.

**Note**:
A short, lightly edited Entry capturing a single thought.

**Tutorial**:
An Entry that walks the reader through reproducible steps.

**Snippet**:
A code-first Entry with minimal prose.

## Curation

**Featured**:
An Item or Project hand-picked for the homepage. Featured Items form "Start here", in a fixed order with no repeated positions.
_Avoid_: Pinned, highlighted

**Tag**:
A lowercase topic label on an Item. An Item has one to five Tags.
_Avoid_: Category, topic

**Draft**:
An Item or Project that is visible only while authoring and never published.
_Avoid_: Unpublished, hidden

**Scheduled**:
An Item dated in the future. It stays unpublished until its date arrives in Nigerian time (WAT).

## Languages

**Translation**:
A Hausa Entry that renders an existing English Entry. The English Entry is the original. Both appear in the archive on their own.
_Avoid_: Version, localization

## Work

**Project**:
A product Ismail has built or is building, shown with its status (live, building, paused, or archived).
_Avoid_: App, portfolio item
