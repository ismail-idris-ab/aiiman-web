This site is run by Ismail Idris. It collects as little as it can.

## What is collected

**Visits.** The site uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to count page views and measure loading speed. They do not use cookies and do not track you across other websites. They record things like the page you visited, your country, device type and browser, but not your name or contact details.

**Newsletter.** If you subscribe, your email address is sent to Buttondown, the service that sends the newsletter. It is used only to send you new posts and updates from this site.

Nothing else is collected. There are no ads, no comment system and no accounts.

## Why

- Page-view counts show which writing is useful.
- Speed measurements keep the site fast on slow and metered connections.
- Your email is needed to send you the newsletter you asked for — your consent is the legal basis, and you can withdraw it at any time.

## Where it is stored

Analytics are processed by Vercel. Newsletter emails are stored by Buttondown. Both are based outside Nigeria, so your data may be transferred abroad.

## Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask to see the data held about you, correct it, delete it, or object to its use. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

## Unsubscribe or delete your data

Every newsletter email has an unsubscribe link. To have your email deleted completely, contact me and I will remove it.
